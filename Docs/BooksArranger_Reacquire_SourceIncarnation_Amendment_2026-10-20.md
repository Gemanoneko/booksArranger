## Same-path replacement is supported without a two-scan ritual

An old original is identified by the exact frozen source membership AND full content SHA, not its pathname or inode alone. After a zero-error complete traversal of the still accessible original configured root, each frozen member must be either (a) positively absent under successful covered parent traversal, or (b) present as an ordinary protected file with fresh native identity/ancestry and a newly computed FULL default-stream SHA different from its frozen old SHA. Native handle/stream/ancestry checks remain existing strict floors. Every present marked member is hashed afresh; cached SHA/stat/metadata is never sufficient, including identical size/mtime. New inode with identical damaged bytes is NOT displacement. Changed bytes prove another incarnation only, never that the replacement is sound. Errors, timeout, unavailable root, changed generation, overlap/unknown member ownership or partial old-content presence hold. No directory/title prefix may widen membership; anthology siblings are unrelated.

Hold the new file and relevant ancestry through observed facts/readback and final same-transaction CAS. Absence has no file handle: retain/revalidate accessible root and successfully traversed nearest existing parent, recheck absence immediately before CAS, and refuse late appearance/unknown coverage. Preserve source disappearance observations as descriptive scan evidence, never deletion authority. All frozen candidate members must be absent/displaced; only an exact whole-source-set match may retire the candidate.
