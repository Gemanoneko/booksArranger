# Books Arranger

Repository: https://github.com/Gemanoneko/booksArranger

Books Arranger helps Sergei inventory and arrange his audiobook, podcast and text-book libraries. It produces verified Apple-compatible audiobooks with source-track navigation and recorded metadata evidence. Automatic public research can approve and enqueue a book only after all independent metadata, exact source scope/order, encoding and cover gates pass; uncertain books are held while other candidates continue. The original scanner milestone remains read-only. The current workflow verifies the audiobook before removing its exact included originals. Verified working files are removed after successful cleanup. Original-track recovery copies remain for 24 hours after source cleanup completes, then expire while the app runs; a selected-book button can permanently delete them sooner. Errors preserve remaining files for recovery. Podcasts, ebooks and unrelated siblings remain untouched. It never invents metadata matches.

## Confirmed rules

- Audiobooks: `Z:\Audiobooks`; `Unsorted` is the arrival area. `Podcasts` is a separate library type and excluded from audiobook grouping.
- Podcasts: `Z:\Audiobooks\Podcasts`. Preserve one episode/file, existing podcast classification, show tags, identifiers, descriptions, artwork and transcripts. Never merge them as audiobooks. Current classification may also reside in iTunes, not just embedded tags.
- Ebooks: `Y:\Books\Books`; preserve file formats and bytes. Arrange `Main Genre/Author/Series if any/Book Title/original file`.
- Ebook main genres: Science Fiction, Fantasy, Horror, Adventure, Mystery & Thriller, Nonfiction, Children's Tales.
- Nonfiction adds a topic between genre and author: `Nonfiction/Topic/Author/Series if any/Book Title`. Psychology, Philosophy, Sexuality, Project Management and Cinema are examples; do not invent a topic absent evidence.
- Original works written in Russian use Russian canonical titles/authors. All other original works use English canonical titles/authors, including Russian translations. Embedded edition language does not establish original language.
- Author folders are surname first. Multi-author series get their series folder at the applicable library root. The review records this explicitly.
- Exception for confirmed multi-author short-story audiobooks: `Z:\Audiobooks\Сборники`, or `Сборники/Series` for a series of collections (example `Пять Вечеров`). The anthology marker overrides the generic root-level multi-author series rule only for this type. Single-author collections, novels, ebooks and podcasts retain existing rules; no guessed anthology classifications or author placeholders.
- Future audiobook `ARTIST` must credit verified author(s), never an assumed reader. The optional `canonical_author` review credit distinguishes ordinary author display from the existing surname-first `author` folder value. Older explicitly reviewed author identity is a visibly labelled fallback. Optional verified `narrator` is appended to future `COMMENTS` alongside all preserved existing comment values; no role is inferred from source Artist/AlbumArtist. This prototype only previews those tags and never rewrites them.
- Future audiobook filenames: `Series - Number - Title - Author.m4b`, with series/number omitted for standalone books. Multi-author ebook series place files directly under their library-root series folder as `Series - Number - Title - Author.original-extension`; preserve their bytes and reject uncertain positions/colliding destinations for review.
- Future output stays within the corresponding source library root. Future authorized cleanup is not implemented in milestone 1.
- Final audiobook target is single-file M4B with appropriate audio, Unicode tags, one cover and optional trustworthy chapters. Actual iTunes/iOS 27 import, search, sync, seek and chapter behavior need a real device pilot before bulk conversion. No device compatibility is claimed by this scanner.
- Incremental delivery follows ComixManagementTool: usable small milestones, preserve completed verified work. Persistent scan continuation is additionally explicit here.

Re-encoding is lossy; matching bitrates does not guarantee bit-identical audio or identical perceived sound.

## English title sorting (2026-10-10)

User confirmed Chapters v2 imports in iTunes when searching the actual title Eye of God; device chapter navigation remains unchecked. Future reviewed non-Russian works use English canonical titles. When that title starts with the whole English word The (case-insensitive, followed by whitespace and a nonempty remainder), keep the full display title and derive a separate Apple title sort value omitting that first word. Theatre, The-Eye, A/An, a lone The, Russian works and unverified language stay unchanged. No filename/album/series rule changes or existing-file retagging.

Metadata preview exposes derived SORTTITLE without a new review field/control. The local index is explicit offline maintenance, not a new startup worker. `openlibrary_index.py` streams the dated works/authors gzip dumps into a sparse, title-covered SQLite generation. Before each resumable slice, native-held sources are authenticated against the dated official Archive metadata's compressed size/SHA1/MD5; full gzip CRC/EOF, positive author identity, SQLite integrity, immutable completion digest/native identity and atomic registry activation are required. A changed source, partial download, malformed JSON/container, unknown SQLite leaf/sidecar, reparse/hardlink or changed completed generation is refused. Matched catalog stubs, unsupported author relationships and missing/unusable referenced authors instead hold their normalized title with a durable reason and schema shape; even another apparently valid same-title candidate cannot make that title decisive. Other independent titles may activate. Requested coverage and eligible coverage remain separate. A pending or refused build preserves the prior active generation. Resuming replays gzip decompression to the committed offset; this saves index work, not decompression time. Lookups only read the activated SQLite and distinguish covered misses, incomplete identity, outside coverage, unavailable and invalid states. They never scan a dump during status, conversion or a book lookup.

Existing negative backfill results get one fair research pass for the expanded source policy. A negative is then bound to the observed local index generation: pending/unavailable data does not spin, but a later valid activation or corrected/expanded generation permits one new pass. DONE, held, skipped and in-progress recovery records keep their existing semantics, and retained preferred Goodreads receipts survive negative research. No live ledger migration/reset is used.
