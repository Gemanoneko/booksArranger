## Finding resolved

**Major, resolved in amendment:** initial design held whenever a replacement occupied the same pathname before a deletion-only scan. That would require an unauthorized two-scan ritual. The amendment treats old content incarnation separately from pathname: every exact frozen original must be either confirmed absent by a complete accessible error-free scan/final recheck, or displaced by a freshly held ordinary source with newly computed full SHA different from the old frozen SHA. Same size/mtime cannot reuse cached hashes; a new inode containing identical damaged bytes does not prove displacement. Changed bytes are not a soundness verdict. No user action must recreate the deletion sequence.
