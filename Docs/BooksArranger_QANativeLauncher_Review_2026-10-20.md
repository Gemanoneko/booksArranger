## Findings

**Major — final pipe output can be lost before a success line.** In native `run`, the loop peeks/drains the pipe, then decides `done` from zero Job membership and the child signal. If the last peek reports zero, the child can emit its final receipt and exit before membership/exit checks; the loop exits without another drain. The watcher then publishes the collected incomplete output and a native success line. Require bounded complete pipe draining after final process-tree closure, reaching a proved broken-pipe/EOF state before success. Preserve byte limits and deadline through that drain. Add native fixtures emitting their final bounded output immediately before exit, including buffered stdout/stderr and inherited-pipe handles; pure schema tests do not exercise this race.

It then compiles into the same exe/obj and uses writeFileSync for the result/manifest. That can overwrite the original NO RESULT artifact or collide with its uncertain invocation. Refuse any reused output namespace. Those are additional behavior even though the command text/raw stdin are retained; an original hook that takes longer or emits more output can now fail. The wrapper PowerShell invocation/environment/exit/signal behavior also needs exact equivalence evidence against the original accepted host entry. Preserve the original effective timeout/output/environment/termination semantics or do not replace that global entry. Prove passthrough with actual controlled fixtures before deployment. Do not call a JSON single-field diff or pure fallback classification a byte-exact behavioral proof.

It chooses one via null-coalescing and can ignore a conflicting alias.
