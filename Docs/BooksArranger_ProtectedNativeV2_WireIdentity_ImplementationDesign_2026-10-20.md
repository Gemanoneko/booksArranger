## Literal terminal wire

Keep the accepted literal format and LF separators, exact order, no CR/BOM/trailing bytes:

payload_base64_bytes=<canonical decimal> LF
<one RFC4648 standard padded base64 line> LF

Zero is 0; other decimals have no leading zero/sign/space.
