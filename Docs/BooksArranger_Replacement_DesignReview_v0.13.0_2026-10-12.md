## Resolved construction blockers

The initial layout would have risked rewriting the parent on restart after the first old-file removal while the other two remained. Revised startup recognizes only exact per-file durable intent/removed state plus native recovery/current-successor proof, independently of overall retirement completion. Unknown missing/changed outputs still fail closed; `_output_valid` never pretends missing old bytes are valid.

The revision concretely freezes atomic admission/current CAS, predecessor fields and distinct-candidate projection. One replacement relation and new ordinary AudioJobs row enqueue together in the same transaction. Current switches only after new output/journal validation and normal new-job staging cleanup. Merely adding the sixth historical row cannot produce six books or remove the existing verified fifth Ready book. Retirement is a serialized normal app postprocessor with Stop boundaries, not an offline lease racing the live app.

Copy and retirement receipts are monotonic with exact full provenance. Stop checks copy chunks and before disposition; shutdown drains owned work/handles. Resume revalidates exact paused intent and never repeats terminal disposal. Unknown errors remain attention rather than automatic replay. Replacement recovery copies stay in their explicit protected owned namespace and unpurged during this initial operation; time alone cannot authorize deletion or generic retention adoption. Any future purge needs its own reviewed provenance/expiry boundary.

## Media contract

Forty ordered original paths/hashes and protected historical frame proof are frozen: 2,828,272,896 frames at 44100 Hz, 64,133.172244897956 seconds, Track 01–40, final endpoint 64,133,172 ms. Execution must freshly establish the same original source bytes/native facts/frame counts. Existing approved source-matched AAC-LC 128000 bit/s, mono, 44100 Hz is reused; no down-rate retry, upmix, 160k reuse or invented file-size cap.

New private artifact requires full decode and recorded fresh source frames, decoded output/source duration within 0.25 seconds, actual bounded chapter readback within 1 ms, correct 65-byte QuickTime/Arial text description, canonical actual tags and one matching cover. Protected bounded supported moov/stsz parsing accounts for actual AAC payload and remaining cover/chapter/container bytes; malformed/unsupported layout/count/size tables refuse. Lead retains validated private identity through publication. Source-matched lossy AAC is not claimed bit-identical or perceptually guaranteed.
