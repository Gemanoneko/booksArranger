## Required migration protocol

1. **Exclusive admission and quiescence.** Define a single migration owner and persistent admission gate blocking scans, conversion, source cleanup, recovery retention, metadata/routing writes, automatic research, retry/repair/replacement and output verifiers where they can change accepted identities. A UI Paused label is not proof that these owners drained. Do not decide unresolved Pause semantics to obtain this state. Snapshot after safe drain and native closure under catalog ownership. Record exact candidate/job/context/output identities and compare-and-swap them at commit.
2. **A finite eligibility manifest.** List every current owned publication selected, with original proof digest, candidate/job/publication kind, old/new roots and paths, routing-policy/evidence versions, bytes/hash and required ancestry. Account separately for corrected/replaced publications, partial cleanup, unknown routing, absent sources, retained duplicates and historical outputs. Ready count is not the selection query. No glob-based migration. Publish exclusions and exact reason; uncertain books remain usable at their existing verified location.
3. **Versioned binding, no provenance rewrite.** Specify relocation schema and uniquely resolve each publication to either historical location or one committed current location. Bind it to immutable original proof, verified destination size/hash, native identity/ancestry and catalog generation. Consumers must reject missing, duplicate, stale, malformed or conflicting bindings. Unmigrated publications continue to resolve; global output-root switch must not strand partial migration. New conversions have separate source and destination roots, and destination validation must not weaken source containment.
4. **Recoverable file/database transitions.** Define durable states for intent, private destination creation, completed copy, destination verification, no-replace publication, catalog binding commit, old-removal intent, old removal and completion. Flush required file/journal/DB state before advancing. Filesystem and SQLite are not one transaction: enumerate crash recovery between each side effect and its receipt. Exact owned destination temporary files only; unknown partial/final files are preserved. After commit, recovery must retain the destination even if cleanup fails. Removal failure is copied-and-verified/original-retained, not moved. Rollback must never discard the sole valid copy.
5. **Revalidate before removal.** Hold validated ordinary ancestry/handles for both paths, reject reparse points/hardlink surprises/unsupported extra streams and changed files, compare full source/destination SHA256 and size, retain supported media validation and exact migration intent. At removal time verify the durable resolver still points to the same destination proof; old path is an exact owned publication, not an original audiobook source. Do not recursively remove author/cycle/universe folders; only separately proved empty owned folders can be considered later.
6. **Windows/NAS failure cases.** Refuse destination collision, including case-folded, Unicode/sanitized-name collision and old/new aliasing; no overwrite even when names resemble known books. Validate free-space budgeting with original retained, bounded copy cancellation/checkpoints, loss/remap of Z:, incomplete writes and unavailable ancestry. Recompute destination identity on restart rather than trusting a drive letter. Preserve unrelated destination contents.
7. **Routing is not proof of identity.** A newly computed hierarchy must use accepted universe/cycle/author-scope decisions. Do not infer multi-author cycle scope from one recording's narrator or current contributors; unknown cycle scope remains held. Collection naming follows the committed UX contract. Changing preview policy must not retroactively mutate frozen approved metadata/job manifests or authorize source cleanup against a new candidate revision.

## Required multi-source preservation

Keep a durable source identity separate from the audiobook library label and output identity. Existing source root retains its IDs, scan checkpoints, grouping revisions, manual-evidence associations and approval. Add OLD_Audiobooks only through validated supported configuration. An unavailable source preserves its catalog rows and reports unavailability; failed enumeration is not evidence of deletion. Ensure scans exclude the output and existing Podcasts boundary, including nested/alias roots. Source-specific grouping and source fingerprint identity must prevent source crossover in refresh/retry/cleanup/restore. Cross-source identical recordings are evidence for duplicate review, not blanket authority to merge candidates or remove either source. All-source counts/pagination must share a coherent snapshot and predicate; do not total root pages or count the same candidate twice.

Persist manual evidence outside disposable canonical draft/hold context. Saving, re-evaluating, lookup, automatic release, Start over, unchanged-source rediscovery and restart must retain it. Changed source identity makes the record stale; it cannot silently attach to a different candidate. Blank unspecified fields must not override provider data, and conflicts require explicit resolution. Routing evidence needs explicit none/unknown distinctions where the UX contract requires them.
