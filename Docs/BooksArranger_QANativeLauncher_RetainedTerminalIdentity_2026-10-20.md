## Documented basis

[Microsoft process termination](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/terminating-a-process) states process objects become signaled on termination and objects remain valid while another process retains an open handle. [WaitForSingleObject](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/synchapi/nf-synchapi-waitforsingleobject) defines WAIT_OBJECT_0 versus timeout/failure and requires SYNCHRONIZE. [GetProcessId](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/processthreadsapi/nf-processthreadsapi-getprocessid) and [GetProcessTimes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/processthreadsapi/nf-processthreadsapi-getprocesstimes) read the process selected by that handle; creation is exact FILETIME and exit data exists for terminated processes. These facts support retained-object provenance; they do not make a numeric PID, error31 or old JSON into ownership.
